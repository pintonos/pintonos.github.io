# Welcome!

Welcome to my updated website. Here, you will find my latest publications, ongoing research projects, curriculum vitae, and updates on my work in machine learning and sequential recommender systems.

## Research Focus

My primary research interests include:
- **Sequential and Session-based Recommendation**: Exploring graph neural networks, contrastive learning, and hypergraph representations to model multi-hop item relations and user intent over time.
- **Music Information Retrieval (MIR)**: Developing semi-supervised multi-relational graph architectures for fine-grained music emotion recognition and recommendation.
- **Machine Learning in Production**: Bridging theoretical deep learning models with scalable, cloud-deployed systems.
